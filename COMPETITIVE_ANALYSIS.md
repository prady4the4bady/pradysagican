## WEAKNESSES IN OUR OWN SYSTEM (Critical Fixes Needed)

### W1: No Unified Wiring — Modules Are Islands
The v1 modules (consciousness, reasoning, memory, world_model) don't talk to v2 modules (stark_core, prometheus, atlas, aegis, nexus). There's no master bus connecting everything.

### W2: No Hallucination Defense
We have no mechanism to verify our own outputs or detect when we're generating false information.

### W3: No Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning
Pure neural approach. No symbolic logic, no formal verification, no knowledge graph-grounded reasoning.

### W4: Multi-Step Reliability Problem
Like ALL agent systems: 95% per step × 20 steps = 36% overall success rate. We need error correction.

### W5: No Temporal Reasoning
Can't reason about time, deadlines, scheduling, or temporal dependencies natively.

### W6: No Multi-Modal Processing Pipeline
Text-only. No vision, audio, video processing pipeline built in.

### W7: No Distributed Execution
Single-machine only. No way to distribute across multiple nodes.

### W8: No Formal Safety Proofs
Safety is rule-based. No mathematical guarantees or formal verification.

### W9: No Real-World Grounding
World model is abstract. No connection to real-world data feeds, sensors, or APIs.

### W10: Modules Can't Compose Dynamically
You can't chain consciousness→reasoning→memory→action in a single flow.
