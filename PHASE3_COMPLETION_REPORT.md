## 📞 Support & Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Q: Evolution system not showing improvements**
A: Give it 24+ hours to collect enough performance data. Initial cycles need baseline.

**Q: Safety blocking too many inputs**
A: Adjust threat patterns in safety_hardening.py or add to whitelist in config.

**Q: Dashboard not loading**
A: Check port 8000 is available. Use `--port 8001` if needed.

**Q: Reasoning taking too long**
A: Reduce number of reasoning methods or debate rounds for faster results.

**Q: Fine-tuning not improving accuracy**
A: Ensure you have enough training data. Try curriculum learning approach.
